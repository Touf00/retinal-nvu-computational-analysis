# Published AP747 brain baseline audit

Louis et al., Angiogenesis 2023 (doi:10.1007/s10456-023-09875-8), reported healthy brain uptake at 120 minutes of 0.02 ± 0.001 %ID in mice (n=3) and 0.037 ± 0.015 %ID in pigs (n=3). These values are percent injected dose, not percent injected dose per gram. This does not establish ischemic brain binding or BBB penetration.
