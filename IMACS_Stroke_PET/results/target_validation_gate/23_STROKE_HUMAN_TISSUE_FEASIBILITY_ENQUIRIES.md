Drafts only, NOT SENT. Confirm host PI, affiliation and ethical governance before requesting physical human tissue. Prepared 13 October 2026. For full endpoints/criteria use 23_HUMAN_VESSEL_PROTEIN_BINDER_VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md.

## Enquiry 1: BRAIN UK

To: brainuk@soton.ac.uk
Subject: Availability enquiry: archived human ischemic-stroke brain FFPE blocks for ICAM1/VCAM1 vascular protein pilot

Dear BRAIN UK team,

I am developing a translational neurovascular imaging project examining whether ICAM1 and VCAM1 proteins are localized to microvascular endothelium in human ischemic brain tissue, as a first step before considering blood-facing binder and PET feasibility experiments.

Before submitting a formal request, could you advise whether the participating NHS neuropathology archives may contain formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) tissue from approximately 8-12 independently documented adult ischemic stroke autopsy cases, preferably with onset-to-death around 12-72 hours? Ideally each case would have neuropathologist-defined infarct core, periinfarct border, and anatomically matched noninfarcted/contralateral tissue, when available. We would also be interested in about 5-8 comparably processed adult nonstroke brain controls. Those numbers are a feasibility planning target, not a claim of statistical power.

We would perform blinded endothelial/mural/leukocyte compartment immunohistochemistry and quantify ICAM1/VCAM1 at the individual-patient level. Could you indicate whether suitable cases and the following de-identified information can be supplied: lesion/hemisphere and sampled region, time from stroke onset to death or best estimate, postmortem interval, fixation details, hemorrhagic transformation, treatment/recanalization, and broad clinical confounders such as severe systemic infection? We can adapt the design to the materials and records that actually exist.

Could you also advise on the appropriate institutional PI requirements, consent/ethics, overseas sample transfer terms, slide/block availability, typical sample retrieval lead times and expected charges? We will submit a full protocol, host details and institutional approvals before any tissue transfer.

Thank you for considering this feasibility enquiry.

## Enquiry 2: AP-HM Centre de Ressources Biologiques, Marseille

To: CRB.Timone.Marseille@ap-hm.fr
Subject: Feasibility: access to neuropathology-confirmed ischemic stroke brain FFPE samples for an ICAM1/VCAM1 vascular study

Dear CRB AP-HM team,

I am developing a planned translational stroke neurovascular imaging research project and would like to ask whether AP-HM, directly or through a clinical neuropathology/pathology collaborator, can support an initial human tissue feasibility study.

The proposed study would examine vascular endothelial ICAM1 and VCAM1 localization by quantitative multiplex immunohistochemistry in archived ischemic infarct tissue. We are especially looking for pathologist-confirmed acute cerebral ischemic infarct FFPE specimens (ideally 12-72 hours after stroke) with infarct/periinfarct/contralateral region identity and minimal de-identified tissue metadata. A pilot target would be around 8-12 independent stroke cases, with matched nonstroke controls where available. We would start with a feasibility inventory and adapt our request to what actually exists.

Could you advise whether ischemic human brain tissue is available through your CRB collection, or whether a neuropathology/autopsy service within AP-HM could be approached? We would also appreciate guidance on the relevant clinical collaborator, ethical and regulatory approvals, tissue transfer conditions and expected histology support.

We are preparing a concise protocol and can send the complete scientific plan and details of the prospective academic host when the appropriate contact is identified. No samples would be requested or processed before the necessary approvals.

Thank you for your assistance.

## Information NOT to claim in outreach

- Do NOT state the samples are already available, that host PIs have approved tissue transfer, or that any human IHC or binder experiment has been performed.
- Do NOT state FFPE ICAM1/VCAM1 intensity proves luminal accessibility or PET tracer specificity.
- Do NOT state a sample size is formally powered: pilot n is subject to biobank feasibility and variance assessment.
- Do NOT share personally identifiable clinical data or tissue photos through the public repository.

Follow-on: if no paired stroke regions are available, ask for histology slides/whole-slide images or nonpaired region blocks ONLY as descriptive evidence. Never invent contralateral pairs.
